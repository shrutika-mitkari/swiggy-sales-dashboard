# Swiggy Sales Dashboard

The Swiggy Sales Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based business analytics project designed to analyze food delivery sales performance, customer ordering trends, restaurant insights, and state-wise sales distribution.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales metrics, ratings, order behavior, and category performance to support business decision-making and operational analysis.

# Business Problem

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze customer ordering behavior
- Track food category demand
- Identify high-performing states and restaurants
- Monitor customer ratings and order trends
- Improve operational and marketing strategies

# Business Insights

- Non-Veg food category contributed the highest share of total sales.
- Karnataka generated the highest state-wise sales revenue.
- Weekend sales were higher compared to weekdays.
- Average customer ratings remained consistently high at 4.34.
- Monthly sales showed stable growth patterns.
- Certain restaurants generated significantly higher order volumes.

# Business Recommendations

- Increase promotional campaigns for top-performing food categories.
- Focus marketing efforts in high-performing states.
- Improve restaurant partnerships in low-performing regions.
- Optimize delivery operations during peak weekend periods.
- Enhance customer retention through loyalty offers and discounts.
